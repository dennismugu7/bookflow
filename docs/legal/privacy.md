# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Bookflow helps salons and barbershops in Kenya take bookings online. It has two parts: a website where clients book (bookflow.mugu-labs.com) and an Android app for salon owners ("Bookflow"). Bookflow is run by Mugu Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

**If you book as a client**
- Your name, email address and phone number, which you give us when you book or sign in (with Google or a 6-digit email code).
- Your bookings: the salon, services, team member, date and time, price, any cancellation reason, and whether you attended.
- Technical data used to keep the service safe: your IP address (kept for one day, to stop automated abuse), and cookies that keep you signed in and hold your chosen time while you book.

**If you use the owner app**
- Your email address, and your Google name and picture if you sign in with Google.
- Your salon's details: name, tagline, description, address and map link, phone, opening hours, services and prices, team members' names and photos, salon photos and logo.
- The client and booking records your salon keeps in Bookflow, including private notes you write.
- A notification token for your phone and your notification settings, so we can send you booking alerts.
- If you delete your account, the reason you choose. We store it without your name or email.

We don't collect your location, contacts, camera or microphone. The app only sees the photos you choose to upload.

## How we use it
- To let clients book, change and cancel appointments, and to show salons their bookings.
- To sign you in and keep your account secure.
- To send emails you need (sign-in codes) and, for owners, booking notifications you've turned on.
- To prevent abuse, for example too many bookings held from one connection.
- To understand and fix problems, and to improve Bookflow.

We don't sell your data. We don't show ads or use your data for advertising.

## Who we share it with
- **The salon you book with.** It sees your name, phone, email and your bookings with it. The salon decides how it uses them for its own business, and is responsible for that use.
- **Service providers that run Bookflow for us**, only as needed:
  - Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage);
  - Vercel (website hosting);
  - Resend (sign-in emails);
  - Expo and Google Firebase (push notifications);
  - Google (sign-in with Google, and map embeds on salon pages);
  - Cloudflare Turnstile (checks that a booking comes from a person).
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers store data outside Kenya. We use providers that protect personal data with appropriate safeguards.

## How long we keep it
- Your account and data are kept until you delete your account, or until a salon deletes its records.
- IP addresses used for abuse protection are deleted after one day.
- When you delete your account we remove your data straight away. Copies in backups are cleared within 30 days.
- When a client deletes their account, the salons they visited keep their visit records (name, phone, bookings) as business records, but no longer linked to the account or the email address.

## Your rights
Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and you can object to how it's used. Most of this you can do yourself:
- **Owners:** in the app, under Menu → Settings → Delete account.
- **Clients:** at bookflow.mugu-labs.com/delete-account.

For anything else, email support@mugu-labs.com. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

## Children
Bookflow is not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell owners in the app when the change is significant.
